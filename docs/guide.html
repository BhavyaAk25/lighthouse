1 · The idea

Lighthouse in one minute

Companies now run several specialised AI agents. Each can be right on its own and still disagree with the others: Sales sees demand, Finance sees a loss, Operations sees a full distribution center, Legal sees a risky claim. Lighthouse is the layer above the agents. Every agent answers in the same format with cited evidence. The department that owns the agent signs off on it, Lighthouse reconciles the four positions, and the executive decides. Every step lands in a tamper-evident log.

3 · The rules

Why it is built this way, and the rules

Ownership

Each department lead owns their agent. They can re-run it, confirm it, change its answer with a reason, or block it. Nobody runs another department’s agent.

Management by exception

A lead only signs when their agent says hold or reject, needs a review, is under 65% confident, or lacks evidence. In the 12 demo cases, about two out of three lanes clear automatically.

Accountability

The packet shows what the AI said and what each person signed. The executive can overrule Sales, Finance or Operations with a written reason. Legal can never be overruled.

A lead must sign when their agent…

  • says Hold or Reject
  • says Review required (for example, Legal flags a claim)
  • is less than 65% confident (admin can change this)
  • is missing evidence (the output is marked invalid, never imputed)

Otherwise the lane shows Cleared automatically. A lead can still step in on any lane.

What a lead can do

  • Confirm the agent’s answer (a note is optional)
  • Adjust it to proceed / with conditions / hold: needs a 20+ character reason, and lowers Lighthouse’s confidence for that agent by 15%
  • Block it: needs a reason. Legal’s version is Reject.
  • Re-run my agent at any time. Its old sign-off is cleared and must be given again.

When the executive can approve

  • Every lane is cleared or signed, and
  • Legal has not rejected it and no legal rule prohibits it
  • A Sales, Finance or Ops block can be overruled with a written reason, and is recorded as an override
  • Going against Lighthouse’s recommendation, or a high-risk case, also needs a reason

Reject and Send back are allowed any time after the agents run.

Deadlines and escalation

  • Each sign-off is due 48 hours after the agents run (admin can change this)
  • An overdue Sales, Finance or Ops lane escalates: the executive may decide without it, with a reason, and the record names who didn’t sign
  • An overdue Legal lane never escalates. Approval stays locked.
Lighthouse reconciles twice: first on the four AI answers, then again on what the departments actually signed. When the signed result differs, the packet says so in plain words: “The AI agents alone said hold (75%). Department sign-offs changed it.”
4 · Getting ready

Before class (10 minutes)

  • Open the live app and sign in as anyone. Click your name (top right): this menu switches person in one click and stays on the same page.
  • In that menu, under Presenter, click Reset demo cases. Any time before class is fine: the demo clock stops 12 hours after a reset, so deadlines never all go overdue.
  • Rules & agents → Agents & AI → Test providers. Both Gemini and Groq should show OK.
  • In the same menu, choose Live AI if the network is good, or Replay if it isn’t (saved AI answers, instant, identical results). The setting applies to everyone in that browser.
  • Tip: give each teammate one persona and pass the laptop, or switch from the name menu. After every sign-off or decision a Next for you card points to the next case, and each desk opens with Since you last looked, so the audience sees every hand-off.

8 · Under the hood

How it works

Agents and their tools

  • Sales: 56-day baseline, seasonality for the exact window, category elasticity, real promo-day analogs from M5, channel mix.
  • Finance: incremental gross profit, basket halo, supplier funding, execution costs, a downside case (−40% uplift), break-even discount solver.
  • Operations: inventory position, launch requirement vs lead time, DC utilization, labor and online fulfillment, projected fill rate.
  • Legal: matches 180 active rules (250 including superseded versions) by category, mechanic, channel, state, discount, duration, flags and claim text.

Pipeline

Tools compute, agents reason, guardrails enforce, people sign, the executive decides. Numbers always come from deterministic tools. The model returns a recommendation, confidence, risk, conditions and cited evidence IDs (schema-validated). Guardrails remove made-up citations and enforce hard limits.

Governance (Deliverable 1 §4.2, extended)

  • Legal prohibition → Reject; nobody can approve it.
  • Missing evidence → invalid output, Review required, never imputed.
  • Operations hard stop, or a Finance hold or reject → no proceed.
  • Support vs oppose → an explicit conflict, never averaged.
  • Confidence = weighted agent confidence × completeness × freshness × (1 − conflict penalty). A human adjustment multiplies that agent’s confidence by 0.85.
  • Department sign-offs as above; then the executive decides.
  • Append-only audit log with a SHA-256 hash chain. Database triggers refuse edits and deletes.
9 · Questions

Likely questions

QuestionAnswer
Why doesn’t every lead review every case?Review fatigue. Leads sign only where their agent has a concern; clean answers clear automatically. Every lane still shows who owns it, and any lead can step in.
Isn’t the human just rubber-stamping the AI?The record says otherwise. Changing an answer needs a reason and is shown side by side with the AI; the Dashboard tracks how often each department confirmed, changed or blocked its agent.
What if a lead never responds?After 48 hours a Sales, Finance or Ops lane escalates and the executive may decide without it, with a reason. Legal never escalates.
Can the executive overrule Legal?No. A Legal rejection or a prohibiting rule locks approval for everyone.
Why is the administrator separate?Separation of duties: the person who sets the rules and thresholds can’t also decide cases under them.
Is this real Walmart data?Demand patterns are real public Walmart data from Kaggle (M5 daily sales; 45-store weekly sales), calibrated to the FY2026 10-K. Stores, inventory, rules and costs are generated and labelled. No Walmart internal data.
What if the AI hallucinates?Numbers come from tools; the AI must cite evidence IDs; unknown IDs are removed; hard limits apply after the AI answers (test T12). The department lead is the last check.
Prompt injection?User text is passed as quoted data, screened for injection patterns, and never treated as instructions (test T13).
Does it meet the 60-second target?Live runs take about 6–15 seconds; a single-agent re-run about 3–5; reconciliation takes milliseconds (test T14).
10 · Help

Troubleshooting

  • A lane says overdue: only D-2026-006 should (it is set up that way). If others do, reset the demo from the name menu.
  • An agent card says “Rules engine”: no AI provider answered. The result is still valid. Re-run, or switch to Replay.
  • Approve is locked: by design. The panel says which department is still open; switch to that person.
  • A demo case is already decided: name menu (top right) → Presenter → Reset demo cases.
  • Need a printout: on any case, Export packet → Print / Save as PDF. It lists every department’s sign-off.

# Lighthouse

**AI Agent Orchestration & Decision Intelligence System** — ISTM 6210 / 4210 Capstone, Fall 2026, George Washington University.

Lighthouse is a governance layer that sits above four department AI agents (Finance, Sales, Operations, Legal). It standardizes their recommendations, validates evidence, surfaces conflicts instead of averaging them away, enforces a legal review gate, and routes every consequential decision to a named human owner with a full audit trail.

Setting: a 50-store Walmart U.S. pilot, calibrated with public Walmart sales data from Kaggle. No Walmart internal data is used; generated records are clearly labelled.
